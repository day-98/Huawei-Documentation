## Health Check

# Objetive 

This function checks and evaluates hardware, OSs, databases, networks, and iMaster NCE-Campus services, helping you learn the health status of the system, detect abnormal check items, and determine whether operational or running risks exist in the system.


Different scenarios (Daily, Pre-upgrade Check, Post-upgrade Check, Pre-expansion Check, and Post-expansion Check) are provided with different check items.

Daily: Select this scenario for health check if iMaster NCE-Campus is slow, unstable, or abnormal.

Pre-upgrade Check: Before upgrading iMaster NCE-Campus or installing a patch, perform this check to identify and eliminate exceptions, ensuring that iMaster NCE-Campus meets upgrade requirements.

Post-upgrade Check: After upgrading iMaster NCE-Campus or installing a patch, perform this check to identify and eliminate exceptions, ensuring that iMaster NCE-Campus will work properly.

Pre-expansion Check: Before expanding the hardware, perform this check to identify and eliminate exceptions, ensuring that NCE meets expansion requirements.

Post-expansion Check: After expanding the hardware, perform this check to identify and eliminate exceptions, ensuring that the software will work properly.
